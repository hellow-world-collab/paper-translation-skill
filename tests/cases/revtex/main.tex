\begin{abstract}
We study the collective motion of self-propelled particles with alignment interactions.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle
Active matter exhibits rich nonequilibrium phases~\cite{smith2020}. We show that
alignment alone suffices to produce giant number fluctuations.
\section{Model}
Each particle $i$ obeys $\dot{\mathbf{r}}_i = \mathbf{v}_i$ with constant speed.
