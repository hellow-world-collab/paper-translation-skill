\begin{frame}{Motivation}
  Policies trained in simulation often fail on hardware because of modelling error.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Actuator delays are ignored in most simulators.
    \item Contact friction is hard to identify.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}{Method}
  We randomise dynamics parameters during training~\cite{lee2021}.
  \begin{block}{Key idea}
    Domain randomisation acts as a form of data augmentation.
  \end{block}
\end{frame}
